Most migration estimates are guesses: a count of dashboards, multiplied by a number of days. An estate assessment replaces the guess with a measured inventory of your whole estate.

## Start with the free *scan*

Every assessment starts with the **free estate scan**. It works from a read-only metadata export: no platform access is required, and a mutual NDA is standard. The scan gives you a headline inventory — how many datasets, calculations and dashboards you have, and how many of them anyone still opens.

## What you *get*

1. **An estate inventory**, with the field-level lineage graph behind it.
2. **A rationalisation recommendation** — what to retire, what to consolidate and what to move.
3. **A migration backlog**, scored for complexity and grouped into waves.
4. **Target considerations** for the BI platforms on your shortlist.
5. **A plan and estimate** you can take to a budget holder.

## Who it's *for*

- Teams approaching a BI platform renewal or a consolidation decision.
- Organisations that inherited several BI platforms through acquisition.
- Data leaders who need a defensible number before committing to a migration.

The assessment is useful on its own. If you do go ahead, it becomes the backlog for the [full migration](/services/full-migration/).
