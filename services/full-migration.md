A full migration takes your estate from the platform you're leaving to the one you've chosen, with the numbers proved to match before anyone switches over. The routes we run most often are [Domo to Power BI](/domo-to-power-bi/) and [Tableau to Power BI](/tableau-to-power-bi/).

## Delivered in *waves*

We don't move everything at once. The estate is grouped into waves by business area and dependency. Each wave is translated, regenerated, tested and cut over on its own, so users move in manageable groups and early waves shape the later ones.

Changes made on the source during the migration are picked up by a re-scan and carried into the specifications, so there's no long development freeze.

## Where people *matter*

In a typical estate, around 78% is converted by our automated migration tool with no analyst in the loop. Our engineers spend their time on the rest: unusual calculations, business logic nobody wrote down, and the conversations about what a metric should mean. Everything is tracked in one backlog, so you can always see what's automated, what's in progress and what's proved.

## What you *keep*

- **Open specifications** in your repository — the platform-neutral record of your estate.
- **Native artifacts** on your chosen BI platform, built the way your own team would build them.
- **A parity report** for every migrated metric.
- **A lineage graph** from every dashboard back to its source columns.
- **A cutover runbook** for each wave.
