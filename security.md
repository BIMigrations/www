## Starting with *metadata*

The free estate scan and most of an estate assessment work from a **read-only metadata export**. It describes your datasets, calculations, dashboards and permissions — not the business data inside them. No access to your BI platforms is needed at this stage.

## Access during a *migration*

When a migration needs access to a source or target platform, that access is:

- **Scoped** to what the current phase needs, and agreed in writing beforehand.
- **Read-only** on the source platform wherever possible.
- **Named**, so you can see who has it and why.
- **Removed** when the phase ends.

## Where your *data* stays

- Open specifications are delivered into **your** repository.
- Regenerated artifacts are deployed into **your** target environment.
- Parity tests compare results for the same slices on source and target. Wherever possible, they run inside your environment.

## Confidentiality

A mutual NDA is standard for every engagement, starting before the first metadata export is shared.

## Reporting a *security issue*

If you believe you've found a security issue affecting BI Migrations, please tell us through our [contact page](/contact/) and choose "Something else". We'll follow up directly.
