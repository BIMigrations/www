## Who we *are*

This website is operated by BI Migrations, LLC ("BI Migrations", "we", "us"). If you have any question about this notice or your personal data, [contact us](/contact/).

## What we *collect*

- **Information you give us.** When you use the estate scan or contact forms, we collect what you enter: typically your name, work email, company, BI platform and message.
- **Preferences stored on your device.** This site uses your browser's local storage to remember whether you chose the light or dark theme. That preference never leaves your device.
- **Fonts.** Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when the page loads.

We don't use advertising trackers on this site.

## How we *use* it

We use the information you send us to reply to you, to arrange and carry out the services you ask about, and to keep a record of our conversation. We don't sell personal data.

## How long we *keep* it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them for the purpose you gave them to us, and then delete them.

## Your *rights*

Depending on where you are, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, [contact us](/contact/). You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
