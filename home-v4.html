§ 02 · Built for your environment

Runs where your data lives.

Air-gapped if you need it

The migration tool runs inside your environment: cloud, on-premises or fully air-gapped. A scan leaves the machine only when you choose to send it.

Private AI, on your premises

The conversion runs on private, secure AI inside your environment, so your estate is never handed to a third-party AI assistant.

Open specifications you own

Your estate becomes SQL, Python, JSON and YAML that you keep, whichever BI platform you choose next.

Parity proven, not promised

Every metric is compared between the old platform and the new one before cutover, with a stated reason for any difference.

§ 03 · Method

Translate once. Target anything.

01

Scan

A read-only crawl inventories every dataset, join, calculated field, chart, dashboard and permission, with usage telemetry that flags what nobody has opened in a year.

02

Translate to open specs

SQL · Airflow / Python · JSON · YAML. Models, transformations and dashboard definitions become plain text in your repository, reviewed like any other code.

03

Regenerate

Our automated migration tool emits native artifacts on the target BI platform, with calculations rewritten in its own language. Automated parity tests then compare every metric against the source before cutover.
