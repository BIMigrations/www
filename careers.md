## How we *work*

- **Evidence first.** Usage data, lineage and parity tests settle arguments, not seniority.
- **Automate the repetitive work.** If we do something twice by hand, it's a candidate for our automated migration tool.
- **Leave things better documented.** Every engagement ends with open specifications the client can actually read.
