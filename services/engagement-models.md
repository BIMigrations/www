## Choosing a *model*

| Model | Best when | Who does the work |
|---|---|---|
| Assessment only | You need a defensible plan and number before committing | Us |
| Delivered migration | You want the outcome, with minimal load on your team | Us, with your sign-off |
| Co-delivery | You want to build in-house capability as you go | Us and your team or partner |
| Tool-assisted | You have a strong team or partner and want automation behind them | Your team or partner, supported by us |

## How it's *scoped*

Every engagement except the assessment is scoped from an estate assessment, so estimates are based on your actual inventory, not a count of dashboards. Models can change between waves. Many teams start with a delivered migration and move to co-delivery once their people know the approach.
